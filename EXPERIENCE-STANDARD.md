# Portfolio Experience Standard | Estándar de Experiencia

## Purpose | Propósito

Every visitor should be able to answer four questions quickly:

1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What does it demonstrate about Eduardo's professional capability?
4. What can I do with the insight, method, or artifact?

## Eduardo's voice | La voz de Eduardo

The portfolio is clear, analytical, practical, and human. It prefers evidence over assertion, systems over superficial fixes, and useful decisions over performative complexity. It is direct without being careless, technical without losing the business context, and candid about uncertainty and limits.

The writing should sound like a professional who cares about rigorous work, people affected by systems, and the operational consequences of security decisions.

## Required content spine | Estructura mínima

Every published item includes:

1. A purpose and intended audience.
2. The problem, question, or decision it addresses.
3. A method, model, or controlled approach.
4. Evidence or sources.
5. Analysis and practical implications.
6. Explicit limitations and what was not demonstrated.
7. Recommendations, next steps, or reusable learning.
8. Authorship, related content, and transparency where relevant.

## Publication quality gate | Puerta de calidad

Before publication, confirm that the item:

- is original or properly attributed;
- has a professional purpose and career signal;
- is useful beyond the original class context;
- contains no credentials, personal data, confidential facts, raw evidence, or protected third-party material;
- is accurate, sourced, and proportionate to its evidence;
- uses consistent naming, metadata, tone, and links;
- provides Spanish and English when the audience justifies both;
- has been checked against [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Content states | Estados

GitHub contains only **published**, **maintained**, or **archived** material. Private ideas and raw drafts stay outside this public repository until they pass the quality gate.
