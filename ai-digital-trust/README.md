# AI & Digital Trust | IA y Confianza Digital

AI adoption changes accountability, autonomy, privacy, security operations, and the relationship between people and systems.

This area evaluates AI with a governance lens: what a system can do, who remains accountable, what evidence supports trust, and how to adopt innovation without normalizing unmanaged risk.

**Expected content:** AI governance, agent accountability, privacy, AI safety, trustworthy automation, digital identity, and responsible adoption.
