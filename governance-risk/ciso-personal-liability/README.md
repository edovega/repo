# Who Answers When the CISO Stays Silent?

[Read in English](README.en.md) · [Leer en español](README.es.md)

> **Purpose | Propósito:** give boards, executives, and security leaders a clear view of the personal legal exposure a CISO carries when an incident is mishandled or hidden.
>
> **Portfolio area | Área del portafolio:** [Governance & Risk](../README.md) · [Catalog](../../CATALOG.md)
>
> **Boundary | Límite:** analysis based on public regulation, public legislative record, and publicly documented cases. No confidential, personal, or third-party material.

**Author | Autor:** Eduardo J. Vega Arguedas (Ed) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eduardovegaa/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/edovega/) · [Program](https://ucenfotec.ac.cr/Carreras/maestria-profesional-en-ciberseguridad-y-seguridad-de-la-informacion/)

This is an executive-oriented article, not an academic submission. It reframes a legal-regulatory analysis as a decision a board, CEO, or CISO should be able to answer before an incident happens, not after.

Este es un artículo de orientación ejecutiva, no una entrega académica. Convierte un análisis jurídico-regulatorio en una decisión que una junta directiva, un CEO o un CISO deberían poder responder antes de un incidente, no después.
