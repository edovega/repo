# Strategy, Leadership & Human Factors | Estrategia, Liderazgo y Factor Humano

Cybersecurity is delivered through people, incentives, trust, communication, culture, and operating decisions.

This area explores leadership and organizational behavior without reducing people to scores or labels. Personality and behavioral models may be discussed as bounded tools for reflection and team design, never as diagnoses or hiring decisions.

**Expected content:** security leadership, culture, human-AI teaming, change management, organizational trust, industrial psychology, and decision-making under pressure.
