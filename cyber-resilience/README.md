# Cyber Resilience | Ciberresiliencia

Cyber resilience is the ability to prepare, continue, recover, and improve when controls fail or disruption occurs.

This area focuses on continuity, recovery, crisis decision-making, dependencies, and measurable readiness. It treats recovery as a business capability, not a document stored on a shared drive.

**Expected content:** continuity and recovery patterns, crisis exercises, ransomware readiness, supplier dependencies, recovery metrics, and resilience frameworks.
