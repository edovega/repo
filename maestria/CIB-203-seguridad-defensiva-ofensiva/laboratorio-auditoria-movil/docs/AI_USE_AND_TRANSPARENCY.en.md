# Generative AI use and academic transparency

## Use statement

This material used **OpenAI Codex** to support document structure, internal-consistency checks, discovery and comparison of public sources, academic-writing review, and agent-assisted peer review.

The laboratory design, test execution, research, analysis, source selection and evaluation, evidence interpretation, arguments, and leadership decisions are the intellectual work of the author, **Eduardo J. Vega Arguedas (Ed)**. Artificial intelligence was not used as a primary source or as a substitute for executed tests. Every technical claim should trace to code, reproducible evidence, or an identified source.

## Applied controls

- Facts, inferences, and recommendations are distinguished.
- Every conclusion and limitation received human review.
- Official or primary links were preferred when available.
- Credentials, personal addresses, financial data, raw screenshots, and private academic records were excluded.
- Local links, shell syntax, SHA-256 integrity, and common sensitive-data patterns were checked before publication.

## Publication boundary

This repository is a sanitized tutorial, not the academic submission or complete evidence archive. Results are presented to teach a reproducible method without disclosing private data or artifacts that could affect third parties.
